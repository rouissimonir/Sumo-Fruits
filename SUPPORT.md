# Sumo Fruits Support

Sumo Fruits: The Bumper Bowl supports iPhone devices running iOS 15 or later.

## Quick troubleshooting

- **No sound:** Check the in-game audio settings and the device volume. Close and reopen the app if audio was interrupted by a call or another app.
- **No haptics:** Enable haptics in the game settings and in iOS. Some devices and system settings may limit haptic feedback.
- **Progress did not load:** Make sure the app has not been deleted or its local data cleared. Progress is stored only on the device and is not synced to an account.
- **Display or controls feel wrong:** Restart the app and include the device model, iOS version, game version, and steps that reproduce the problem when reporting it.

## Contact support

Report reproducible issues through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/rouissimonir/Sumo-Fruits/issues). Do not post personal, account, or sensitive information in a public issue.
