# Playtest research

No stranger sessions have been recorded yet. Do not label a self-play session as a stranger playtest.

For each of five new mobile puzzle players, note the date, device, first-launch or returning state, time to first launch and first merge, levels reached, where they stopped, and visible points of confusion. Ask afterward: **What was the goal? What was the best moment? Would you play again tomorrow?** Keep answers in their words.

Only record or share the player's face, voice, or hands with their permission. The local session log exports gameplay events without an account or identifying fields.
